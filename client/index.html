01 · The Game

Own the floor,
the wall and the sky.

Up to 20 drivers per arena on server-authoritative 60 Hz physics. One shared map, four ways to drive it: floor, wall, ceiling or air.

01 Hover

Take altitude on command. The sky-taxi servo holds your climb dead-steady and bleeds it gently when you let go — park on a rooftop if you want.

02 Grip

A magnetic-fan pin that drives loops, wall bands and ceiling runs. The ride carries its momentum through the curl's steep band instead of sliding off.

03 Boost & flips

Shift for a cooldown-gated burst, and flick the mouse in the air for front/back flips. Boost is the only way over the tall crest.
